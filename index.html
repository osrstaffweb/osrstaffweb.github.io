GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
begin.

Here are a few things worth knowing before starting a project with OSR Digital Solutions.

What can OSR Digital Solutions help me build?

We can help plan and create business websites, online stores, and custom digital experiences. We also connect customers with our specialist domain and SEO platforms.

Are OSR Domains and OSR SEO Solutions separate websites?

Yes. They're distinct, purpose-built storefronts within the OSR ecosystem, with their own products and services.

Can I start with a small project?

Absolutely. A focused website or specific digital need is often the best starting point. The scope can grow as your business does.

Is the shared OSR account a working login system?

No. This is a design demonstration, not a live authentication system. For production, both shops should use OpenID Connect with a central identity provider such as Keycloak. Never enter a real password in this prototype.
